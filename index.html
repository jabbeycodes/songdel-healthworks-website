Compliance-minded by design

Access only what the role requires.

The operating model is designed to support client-specific HIPAA obligations through workforce training, contractual safeguards, controlled access, and documented response procedures.

Unique credentials & MFA

No shared accounts. Access is attributable to the individual and approved for the assigned role.

Minimum necessary access

System access does not equal permission. Personnel use only the records and functions needed for authorized work.

Controlled work environment

Company-managed endpoint strategy, screen locking, endpoint protection, and restrictions on local storage, printing, screenshots, USB, and personal cloud tools.

Audits, remediation & rapid offboarding

Documented training, quality reviews, incident escalation, retraining, and prompt access termination.

Final contractual, privacy, security, employment, and cross-border arrangements remain subject to review by qualified U.S. and Ghanaian counsel and to each client's own policies.

Questions, answered clearly.

Are Songdel professionals independent U.S. clinicians?

No. A Ghana-based physician, nurse, or other healthcare professional does not independently practice medicine or nursing in the United States unless they separately hold every required U.S. license and authorization. Songdel roles are defined operationally, supervised appropriately, and bounded by approved protocols.

How is Songdel different from a general VA provider?

The model starts with healthcare selection, a six-week Academy, role-specific competency, client workflow training, progressive access, and ongoing quality review. Specialty tracks begin with cardiology and can expand as playbooks mature.

Can a practice begin with one workflow?

Yes. A focused pilot—such as referral processing, scheduling, records acquisition, or prior-authorization tracking—creates a clear baseline and a safer path to broader support.

How is performance measured?

Measures are chosen with the client and may include accuracy, task turnaround, rework, queue closure, escalation quality, reliability, patient or staff experience, and time returned to domestic teams.

What happens after Academy graduation?

Graduation alone does not grant independent production access. The role, systems, and access level must be approved, followed by progressive work under supervision and structured 30-, 60-, and 90-day quality review.
