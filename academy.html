How learning works

One academy, one learning system.

Every course, unit, and exam runs through the Songdel Academy LMS. Trainees follow a defined learning path, instructors track each cohort’s progress, and daily quizzes, skills checks, and case reviews sit alongside the weekly examinations — nothing is left to chance or memory.

Training is a gate — not a guarantee.

Attendance is 100% expected, missed core modules are made up before progression, and certain privacy, security, patient-safety, or integrity failures trigger immediate remediation regardless of score. Every assessment and sign-off lives in the trainee’s competency file.

Daily

Short quizzes, skills checks, or case reviews keep every trainee’s level visible, every day.

Weekly

A formal written and practical examination closes each week before the next one opens.

Curriculum

Six weeks, six outcomes.

Each week ends in a demonstrable outcome — not just covered material.

WEEK 01

HIPAA, privacy, security & conduct

Safe to enter a U.S. healthcare environment. This week is a gate: no EHR access until it is passed.

WEEK 02

The U.S. healthcare system

How outpatient care, insurance, eligibility, referrals, and the revenue cycle actually work.

WEEK 03

PulsePoint & cardiology fundamentals

The client, its services, the patient journey, and clear escalation boundaries.

WEEK 04

eClinicalWorks boot camp

Navigate eCW and execute core workflows — scheduling, documents, messages, tasks — accurately.

WEEK 05

VA operations boot camp

End-to-end simulated clinic days across multiple queues, at real operational pace.

WEEK 06

Practicum & certification

Supervised live work that ramps up through the week, then final exams and readiness sign-off.

Week 6 is earned, not scheduled.

Real practice work begins only after every prior gate is passed and role-based access is approved — starting with lower-risk administrative workflows under close supervision.

Certification

Three levels of readiness.

Certification describes exactly what a graduate is approved to do — nothing more, nothing assumed.

LEVEL I

Songdel Academy Graduate

Completed the classroom curriculum. Not yet independently production-ready.

LEVEL II

Certified Songdel Healthcare VA

Passed examinations, simulations, and the practicum — approved for a defined production role.

LEVEL III

Songdel Advanced Clinical VA

Additional competency in advanced pre-charting, clinical support, prior authorization, quality, and specialty workflows.

And after graduation?

Access stays role-based and progressive, with structured quality review at 30, 60, and 90 days — the Academy’s standards follow every graduate into production.

Academy questions.

How long is the program?

Six weeks — 30 training days and roughly 240 hours — full-time and intensive, with daily assessments and a formal examination every week.

How is training delivered?

Through the Songdel Academy LMS: structured courses and units on a defined learning path, with instructor guidance and cohort progress tracked throughout.

Do I need a healthcare background?

Yes — the Academy builds on an existing healthcare foundation (nursing, midwifery, medicine, health administration, records, or allied health). It teaches U.S. practice operations; it does not replace clinical training.

What happens after I apply?

Shortlisted candidates are contacted for the next stage of selection. Cohort places are limited, and progression through the Academy itself is gated by the standards on this page.

Does graduation mean I start work immediately?

Graduation is Level I. Production work begins at Level II — after examinations, simulations, and the supervised practicum — in a defined role, with 30/60/90-day quality reviews.
